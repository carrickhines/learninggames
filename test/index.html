📝 Practice Test

This works the way the test at school works. It is not a game, and that is the point — so that the real one on the day is not a surprise.

Which test?

Which level?

A whole test is 43 questions and takes half an hour or more, with no clock. You can stop any time by closing the page — nothing is lost, and nothing is scored until the end.

Before you start

There is no clock. Take as long as you like on every question. Nobody is timing you and nothing runs out.

You cannot go back. Once you press Go On, that question is finished. You can change your mind as many times as you like before you press it.

Some questions will be too hard. That is not a mistake and it is not your fault — the test keeps making questions harder until it finds the ones you cannot do yet. That is how it works out what you know. Getting some wrong means it is working properly.

Answer every one. If you do not know, get rid of the answers that are definitely wrong and pick from what is left. A guess is better than nothing.

🔊 Every question will be read out to you. Tap the speaker to hear it again as many times as you want. Headphones are a good idea.
